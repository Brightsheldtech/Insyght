# Insyght — Project Instructions

## Writing style rules (apply to ALL copy changes)

- **No unnecessary hyphens or dashes in visible text.** This includes em dashes (—), compound adjective hyphens where the meaning is clear without them, and list separators. Write "plain English" not "plain-English", "White label" not "White-label", "cross referenced" not "cross-referenced", "AI powered" not "AI-powered".
- Write as someone who understands how Nigerian business actually works — specific, direct, no filler.
- Never use em dashes (—) as parenthetical separators. Rewrite as separate sentences instead.

## Business context

Insyght is a Nigerian company intelligence platform built by BrightSheld Technologies.
It delivers 11-section due diligence reports on any CAC-registered Nigerian company in under 2 minutes.
Target customers: procurement officers, lawyers, banks/fintechs, investors.
Key differentiator: sector licensing checks (CBN, NAFDAC, SEC, SCUML) that competitors don't offer.
